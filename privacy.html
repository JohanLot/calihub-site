CaliHub Privacy Policy

Company & website policy

This page covers CaliHub as a company and the calihub.org website — not any individual app. Our short version: we keep no information about you, and we do not sell or share anything with third parties.

What this page covers

Each app we build has its own privacy policy, because different apps collect different information depending on what they do. This page is separate from those — it's about CaliHub the company, and about this website itself.

Information we collect on this website

None, automatically. This site doesn't use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts of any kind. The only information we ever receive is whatever you choose to send us directly — for example, if you email support@calihub.org, we see your email address and whatever you write.

Sharing and selling data

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers, for any reason. There are no exceptions to this.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set tracking cookies and does not use third-party analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. It's a short policy because our practice is simple: we don't collect what we don't need.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@calihub.org.
