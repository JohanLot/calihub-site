Rough-In

In development

A permanent home record, organized by room and system — not by project. Rooms and systems like the Kitchen, HVAC, or Electrical Panel never "close" the way a renovation tracker does; every repair, upgrade, and maintenance visit just becomes another entry in that room's ongoing history.

How it works

One running record per room and system

  • Room & system profiles that accumulate history over time instead of closing when a project wraps up.
  • Photo-log with context — capture a photo, tag it to a room or system, add a one-line note. Built for documenting wiring, plumbing, and structure before it's covered by drywall or flooring — the rough-in moment, on record.
  • Maintenance reminders scoped to the system they belong to — HVAC filter every 3 months, water heater flush yearly — not a generic to-do list.
  • Document vault for permits, warranties, manuals, and invoices, attached to the room or system they actually belong to.
  • Contractor log — who did the work, contact info, and cost, so you know who to call again.
  • Multiple houses for anyone building or maintaining more than one property at a time.

Rough-In isn't a project tracker — there's no "done" state for a room. A kitchen renovated in 2024 and re-plumbed in 2026 shows both, in order, in one continuous record.

When it's time to sell, export a clean summary of the house's full history, or hand the new owner a transfer file that brings every room, photo, document, and reminder directly into their own account.

Rough-In is functionally complete and currently being prepared for release on Google Play.
