Start here

Getting started

When you first open Rough-In and sign up, you'll be asked to set up your house — this becomes the container for every room, system, and record you track from then on.

  1. Enter a house name (e.g. "123 Main St") and, optionally, its address.
  2. Tap Create House.
  3. You're dropped straight into an empty house, ready for its first room or system.

Already have a Rough-In record for this house — say, you just bought it? Tap "Bought a house with an existing record? Import it" instead of creating a new one. See Export & transfer.

The permanent record

Rooms & systems

Rooms (Kitchen, Bedroom, Garage) and systems (HVAC, Electrical Panel, Roof) are the permanent containers your history lives in. Unlike a project tracker, they never "close" — a kitchen renovated in 2024 and re-plumbed in 2026 shows both, in order, in one continuous record.

  1. On the Home tab, tap the + button.
  2. Choose Room or System, then tap a preset (Kitchen, Bathroom, HVAC, Roof, and more) — or type a custom name.
  3. Tap a preset again for a second one of the same kind (e.g. a second "Kids Bedroom") — Rough-In numbers it automatically.
  4. Long-press a room or system on the Home tab to delete it (and everything logged under it).
The core habit

Logging photos & notes

This is the habit Rough-In is built around: documenting wiring, plumbing, and structure before it's covered by drywall or flooring — so you always know what's behind the wall.

  1. Open a room or system, then tap the + button.
  2. Take Photo or Choose Photo from your library — or skip the photo and just add a note.
  3. Add a one-line note (e.g. "Sink shutoff valve, behind drywall before tile").
  4. Tap Save Entry — logging one should take about 10 seconds.

Tap any entry with a photo to view it full-screen. Long-press an entry to move it to another room/system or delete it.

Not a to-do list

Maintenance reminders

Reminders are scoped to the system they belong to. An HVAC filter reminder lives on the HVAC system; a gutter-cleaning reminder lives on the Roof.

  1. Open a room or system, tap the bell icon, and fill in a title, how often it repeats (e.g. "3 months", "1 year"), and the next due date — typed as MM/DD/YY (just type digits, the slashes are added for you).
  2. On the Reminders tab, tap Mark done when you've done the work — it advances the due date by the repeat interval, starting from today.
  3. Already marked done today? The button shows Completed and greys out until tomorrow, so you can't accidentally mark it done twice.
  4. Tap a reminder's title/date (not the button) to edit it or delete it.
Not a generic files folder

Document vault

Permits, warranties, manuals, and contractor invoices — attached to the room or system they actually belong to.

  1. On the Documents tab, tap +.
  2. Choose a file, then attach it to a specific room/system — or to the whole house, for things like a deed or an insurance policy.
  3. Tap a document to open it. Long-press to move or delete it.
Who to call again

Contractors

Keep a simple log of who did the work — useful for warranty claims, or for remembering who to call again.

  1. On the Contractors tab, tap + and add a name, specialty, phone, and email.
  2. Tap any contractor to edit their details, or delete them.
Building more than one?

Multiple houses

Building or maintaining more than one property at a time? Each house keeps its own completely separate record.

  1. Tap the house name pill at the top of the Home tab to switch between houses, or add another.
  2. Your first house is always free. Adding a 2nd (or importing one) needs Rough-In Pro.
Selling, or handing it off

Export & transfer

From Settings, there are two different exports — pick the one that matches what you need:

  • Export House Record — a clean, readable PDF of the house's full history. Hand it over at resale, for an insurance claim, or a home inspection.
  • Export for Transfer — a data file that a new owner can import directly into their own Rough-In account, bringing every room, photo, document, and reminder with it. Use this when you're selling the house.

On the receiving end: the new owner installs Rough-In, then taps "Bought a house with an existing record? Import it" and selects the transfer file.

One-time purchase

Rough-In Pro

A one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks:

  • Unlimited houses — track as many properties as you're building or maintaining.
  • Export — both the PDF record and the transfer file.

Bought it already on another device, or reinstalled the app? On the Pro screen, tap Restore Purchases — since Pro is tied to your account, it's usually already unlocked automatically the moment you sign back in.

Housekeeping

Settings & account

  • Edit a house — tap Edit next to the house name in Settings to rename it or change its address.
  • Delete a house — Settings → Danger zone → Delete House. This is permanent and removes every room, photo, and document under it. You'll need to type your email to confirm.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete My Account. Permanently removes your account and every house you own. Also requires typing your email to confirm.
