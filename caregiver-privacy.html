CareGiver Privacy Policy

Draft — not yet final

CareGiver is a shared activity feed that helps families coordinate care for an aging parent. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

1 Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, used to sign you in and identify you within a family circle.
  • Content you log — caregiving actions you record (calls, doctor visits, groceries, bills paid), coverage requests, and appreciation "hearts" you send.
  • Circle membership — which family circle(s) you belong to, and who else is in them.
  • [Confirm: any device/technical data collected, e.g. push notification tokens for digest/coverage-request alerts?]

2 How we use it

  • To show your circle a shared feed of caregiving activity.
  • To generate the weekly digest recap.
  • To deliver coverage requests and appreciation hearts to the right circle members.
  • To keep your account secure and restore your session when you reopen the app.

We do not use your content to train models, and we do not use it for advertising.

3 Visibility within your circle

CareGiver is built around shared visibility, so it's worth being explicit about who sees what: anything you log — an action, a coverage request, a heart — is visible to every member of the same family circle. It is not private between you and the app; it is private to your circle only, not to the public or to other CareGiver users outside it.
