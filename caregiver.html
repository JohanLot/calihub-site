CareGiver

In development

A shared activity feed for families coordinating care for an aging parent. When care falls unevenly on one sibling — usually geography or circumstance, not choice — the rest of the family often can't see how much is actually happening. CareGiver closes that visibility gap. It doesn't rebalance the workload; it makes sure everyone sees the same picture.

How it works

One shared picture, no guesswork

  • Anyone in a family "circle" can log a caregiving action in one tap — a call, a doctor visit, groceries, a bill paid.
  • Everyone in the circle sees the same shared feed. No guilt-tracking, no leaderboard — just "here's what's happening."
  • A weekly digest recaps the week for anyone who doesn't check daily.
  • A low-friction way to ask the circle for coverage on a specific need.
  • Small appreciation "hearts" to acknowledge what someone else logged.

CareGiver is a coordination tool, not a medical records or care-plan system — it's built to make caregiving visible to a family, not to store or manage clinical information.

CareGiver is already built and in final testing ahead of release on Google Play.
